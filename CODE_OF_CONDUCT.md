# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this to be a welcoming space for anyone curious about hard mathematics — students, hobbyists, researchers, and the merely fascinated. Everyone is welcome to ask questions and propose ideas.

## Expected behavior

- **Be respectful.** Critique mathematics, not people. "This step is unjustified because…" is great; "you're an idiot" is not.
- **Be honest.** Don't overstate results. Clearly mark what is proven vs. conjectured vs. guessed.
- **Be constructive.** When you point out a flaw, explain it and, if you can, suggest a direction.
- **Assume good faith.** Most people submitting flawed ideas are sincere and learning. Meet them with patience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Bad-faith spam, plagiarism, or misrepresenting others' work as your own.
- Aggressively insisting a refuted argument is correct after clear explanation (repeatedly reopening closed threads).

## Reviewers' commitment

Rejections and "this doesn't hold up" will always come with a *reason* and, where possible, a pointer to learn more. We close flawed proofs **with kindness**, never with mockery.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove contributions and comments that violate this code, and may block repeat offenders. Report issues to the repository maintainer.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
